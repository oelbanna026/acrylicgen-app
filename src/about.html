About Acrylic Designer Pro

Laser cutting-friendly DXF/SVG generator for acrylic, MDF, and similar sheet materials.

← Back to App

What this tool does

  • Generate vector files (SVG/DXF) that are ready for laser cutting.
  • Create parametric shapes, layouts, and box designs.
  • Support common production needs like scaling, nesting, and cost estimation.

Accuracy & laser cutting

Laser cutting results depend on your machine, material, and kerf. Always do a small test cut before mass production. For tab & slot (finger joints), you usually need a tolerance/clearance (e.g. 0.15–0.30mm depending on material).
